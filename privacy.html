Vitruvius

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

The short version

  • Vitruvius is a small, invite-only workout tracker run by one person (the "app owner"). It is not a company.
  • We store the workouts, weigh-ins and notes you log so you can see your history. Other members can't see them.
  • We don't show ads, don't use analytics or tracking, and never sell or share your data.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time.

What we store

Your account

  • Your email address and the time you last used the app.
  • Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. It is stored in scrambled (hashed) form, and neither the app nor the app owner can see it.
  • Whether your email address has been confirmed.

What you log

  • Workouts: date, exercises, sets, weights, reps, cardio minutes, how long the workout took, and any notes you write.
  • Body weight entries and their dates.

The invite list

  • The email addresses the app owner has invited, including yours.

On your device only

Some things are kept in your browser's storage on your phone or computer and are not sent to our database: a workout you're in the middle of logging, the workout timer, the last exercise you viewed on Progress, whether you've seen today's welcome screen, and your WHOOP connection (if you connect one). Signing out clears these from that device, except the welcome-screen date.

WHOOP (optional)

If you choose to connect WHOOP, the app asks WHOOP for permission to read your recovery, cycle (strain), sleep and profile data, and uses it to show your recovery score. You can disconnect at any time from the account menu.

  • Your WHOOP access keys are stored only in your browser on that device, not in our database.
  • To talk to WHOOP securely, the app sends requests through a small relay server run by the app owner on Cloudflare. Who can see your data

  • You. The database only lets a signed-in member with a confirmed email read or change their own workouts and weigh-ins.
  • Not other members. Members cannot see each other's data, or even who else has an account.
  • The app owner, technically. As the person who runs the app's Firebase project, the app owner can view the database through Google's admin console, for example to fix a problem. They won't look at your data except to help you or keep the app working, and won't share it with anyone.
  • Google, which stores the data on the app owner's behalf (see below).

Services the app relies on

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore): stores your account and everything you log, and sends sign-up confirmation and password reset emails.
  • GitHub Pages: serves the app's files (the page, images and code). Like any website host, it may record basic connection details such as your IP address.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr: supply the app's fonts and charting code. Your browser downloads these directly, so these services see your IP address.
  • Cloudflare and WHOOP: only if you connect WHOOP (see above).

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. None of them receive your workouts or weigh-ins except Google Firebase, which stores them.

How your data is used

Only to show you your own history, progress and summaries inside the app. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or anything else, and it is never sold or shared.

Deleting your data

  • Delete everything: open the account menu (the person icon, top right), enter your password under Delete account, and tap twice to confirm. This permanently removes your workouts, weigh-ins, account record and login. It can't be undone.
  • Your email stays on the invite list until the app owner removes it. Ask them if you'd like it removed.
  • If the app owner removes your invite, you lose access to the app, but your data is not deleted. Ask the app owner if you want it deleted in that case.

Security

All connections use encryption (HTTPS). Database rules limit each member to their own data, and anyone who isn't a signed-in, invited member is refused. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

Not medical advice

Vitruvius is a logbook. Nothing in it, including recovery scores and estimated one-rep maxes, is medical or professional advice.

Age

Vitruvius is not intended for anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change. For significant changes, the app owner will let members know directly.
